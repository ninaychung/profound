Is each change real or noise?

Our samples are small, so a change we see might just be luck. This section checks how much to trust each change.

  • Plausible range (95% interval): the range the true change most likely sits in, given how few answers we have. A wide bar means a small sample.
  • If the range crosses 0, we cannot rule out that nothing changed. The bar is faded and the verdict says "could be 0".
  • p-value: the chance of seeing a gap this big if nothing had really changed. Below 0.05 is the usual bar for "statistically significant". It is calculated per engine.
